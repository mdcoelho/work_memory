# User Pains

## Evidence base

These pains are extracted from repository documentation and treated as first-hand evidence from the founder's work.

## Pain clusters

### Memory pain

The founder loses details of what happened, why it happened, and what state the work was in.

### Recovery pain

The founder wastes energy returning to work after a context switch.

### Reporting pain

The founder struggles to explain progress because the evidence of work is distributed across containers.

### Handoff pain

The founder cannot hand off work cleanly when decisions and reasoning were never captured.

### Tool-friction pain

The founder works around containers that are optimized for artifacts, obligations, documentation, time records, and team process rather than context identity.

## Strongest pain

The strongest documented pain is the gap between productive work and remembered work.

The founder changes contexts, answers questions, makes decisions, and touches many artifacts. Later, the founder must reconstruct the context graph from scattered traces.
