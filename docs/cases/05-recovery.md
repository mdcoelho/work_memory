# Recovery

## Scope

Recovery is every moment where the founder tries to remember work context.

The founder recovers context from memory, Outlook, Odoo, Fusion, documents, meetings, notes, and task traces.

## Remembering what happened yesterday

Today, recovery starts by scanning yesterday's messages, calendar events, documents, Odoo records, Fusion activity, and memory.

The founder reconstructs a sequence of work fragments. The hard part is not finding a single artifact. The hard part is connecting the artifacts into the story of what happened.

## Remembering why a decision was made

The founder looks for the conversation, record, model state, or meeting that shaped the decision.

Recovery fails when the decision exists but the reasoning between tools was never captured.

## Remembering which workstream this belonged to

The founder works across multiple workstreams that coexist inside the same day and sometimes inside the same software.

Outlook, Odoo, and Fusion can each show an item without clearly explaining which workstream the founder was advancing.

## Remembering how much time was invested

The founder values Work Logs over timers because the important question is usually not only duration.

When time matters, the founder reconstructs effort from the sequence of work: messages answered, records checked, Fusion reasoning, supplier follow-up, management questions, and meetings.

The gap is meaning. Duration alone cannot explain what the time produced.

## Remembering where work stopped

The founder recovers the stop point by reopening the relevant tool and rebuilding the prior mental state.

Examples:

- Outlook shows the last message but not the answer forming in the founder's head.
- Odoo shows the record but not why it was being checked.
- Fusion shows the model but not the business or supplier context that triggered it.
- A meeting note shows a decision but not which interrupted thread it affected.

## Remembering what to report

The founder reports by reconstructing completed work.

Recovery involves pulling together progress, decisions, blockers, supplier context, Odoo checks, Fusion work, and management questions.

## How recovery happens today

1. Identify the workstream from memory.
2. Search Outlook for the communication trail.
3. Check Odoo for business state.
4. Reopen Fusion for technical state.
5. Review documents or notes.
6. Recreate the reasoning between tools.
7. Decide where to resume or what to report.

## Where recovery fails

- The communication trail exists but the reasoning is missing.
- The business record exists but the trigger is missing.
- The model exists but the decision context is missing.
- The meeting happened but its effect on other workstreams is unclear.
- The founder remembers effort but not enough detail to explain it cleanly.

## Recovery principle

Recovery should start from captured context, not from searching every tool.
