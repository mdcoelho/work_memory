# Reporting

## Current reporting behaviour

The founder reports work by reconstructing the day from scattered traces.

The report is assembled from:

- Outlook conversations.
- Odoo checks.
- Fusion work.
- Supplier questions.
- Management messages.
- Meetings.
- Documents.
- Memory.

The founder does not simply report planned tasks. The founder reports what actually happened: decisions, progress, blockers, supplier status, business checks, and technical reasoning.

## How reporting happens today

1. A management question or reporting need appears.
2. The founder stops active work.
3. The founder searches recent Outlook messages.
4. The founder checks Odoo for business state.
5. The founder recalls Fusion reasoning or design state.
6. The founder reviews notes, documents, and recent memory.
7. The founder assembles a narrative manually.
8. The founder returns to the interrupted work with less context than before.

## Why reporting is hard

Reporting is hard because the evidence is real but disconnected.

Outlook shows communication. Odoo shows business records. Fusion shows design state. Meetings create decisions. None of these tools explain the workday as a professional memory.

The founder has to connect the evidence manually.

## What the report needs to contain

- What work was done.
- Which workstream it belonged to.
- Why the work mattered.
- Which supplier, product, customer, or management question triggered it.
- What was decided.
- What remained blocked.
- What needs to resume next.
- How much effort was invested, when duration matters.

## Current failure modes

- The founder remembers the obligation but not the completed work.
- The founder remembers the tool but not the reason for opening it.
- The founder remembers the conversation but not the Odoo or Fusion state behind it.
- The founder remembers the model or record but not the management explanation.
- The founder estimates work from memory instead of using a reliable work record.

## Ideal reporting behaviour

The founder should report from accumulated work memory, not from reconstruction.

The ideal reporting experience behaves like this:

1. Work context is captured during the day.
2. Entries preserve decisions, blockers, reasoning, and resume points.
3. Work remains connected across Outlook, Odoo, Fusion, meetings, and supplier questions.
4. The founder reviews what happened.
5. The founder produces a clear report from actual work context.
6. The report does not force the founder to relive the whole day.

## Reporting principle

The report should emerge from the Work Log.

It should not depend on the founder searching every tool and rebuilding the story from memory.
