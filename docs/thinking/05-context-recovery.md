# Context Recovery

## Definition

Context recovery is the process of becoming mentally present inside a previous context again.

It is not reopening the right file. It is not finding the right record. It is not reading the last message.

Those actions help only when they restore meaning.

## Three-week return

If the founder returns after three weeks, the first need is not a chronological dump.

The founder needs immediate orientation:

- What is this context?
- Why did it matter?
- Who or what is involved?
- What problem was active?
- What changed recently?
- What decision was made or pending?
- What artifacts matter and why?
- What was unresolved?
- Where did the context stop?
- What should be considered before continuing?

## What should appear immediately

The founder should see the active shape of the context:

- Current summary.
- Last meaningful state.
- Key relationships.
- Important decisions.
- Open questions.
- Relevant constraints.
- Recent changes.
- People or organizations involved.
- Artifacts that matter.
- The safest resume point.

This is not about showing everything. It is about showing enough to regain competence.

## What the founder should not need to remember

The founder should not need to remember:

- The old temporary name of the context.
- Which artifact originally triggered the work.
- Which container contained the last clue.
- Why a decision was made.
- Which options were rejected.
- Which supplier, customer, product, or meeting affected the situation.
- What had changed since the previous session.
- Whether the context was paused, split, merged, resolved, or abandoned.

The product should carry that continuity.

## Recovery versus search

Search finds artifacts.

Recovery restores meaning.

Search can answer "where is the thing?" Recovery answers "what is going on here?"

The founder needs recovery more than search because the artifacts usually exist. The missing piece is the thread between them.

## Recovery versus history

History shows events.

Recovery shows relevance.

The founder should not have to replay every event to understand the present state of a context. The product should preserve how the context arrived at its current state and what matters now.

## Recovery success

Recovery succeeds when the founder can continue, explain, or decide without rebuilding the context from scratch.

The founder feels oriented, not merely reminded.
