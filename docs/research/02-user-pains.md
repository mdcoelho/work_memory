# User Pains

## Research status

This document extracts pains already described or implied in the repository.

These pains are not validated. They are candidates for research.

## Pain clusters

### Memory pain

The user may lose details of what happened, why it happened, and what state work was in.

Research question: What memory failures cause real consequences rather than mild inconvenience?

### Recovery pain

The user may waste energy returning to work after a context switch.

Research question: What does successful recovery look like from the user's point of view?

### Reporting pain

The user may struggle to explain progress because the evidence of work is distributed.

Research question: Who asks for updates, what format is expected, and what information is missing today?

### Tool-friction pain

Existing tools may be too structured, too collaborative, too slow, or aimed at the wrong job.

Research question: Which tool is closest to solving the pain today, and why does it still fail?

## Assumptions and questions

| Assumption | Questions to answer before product decisions |
| --- | --- |
| Context loss is more painful than task loss. | In recent examples, was the pain caused by forgetting the task, the reasoning, the state, or the next action? |
| Work history matters more than time duration. | When users look back, do they need meaning, time, outcomes, accountability, or billing evidence? |
| The user wants less structure at capture time. | Do users avoid structure because it is slow, or do they need some structure to trust retrieval? |
| The user wants to remember "what actually happened." | What counts as "what happened": activity, decision, communication, artifact, blocker, outcome, or emotional state? |
| The pain occurs daily. | How many times did the user experience it yesterday? Last week? |
| The pain is severe enough to change behavior. | What workaround does the user use today? What have they paid for or adopted? |

## Unknowns

- Which pain is strongest.
- Which pain is most frequent.
- Which pain users already pay to solve.
- Whether the pain is individual, managerial, or team-level.
- Whether the pain is caused by tools, habits, workload, or organizational culture.

## Contradictions to investigate

- The docs reject task management, but users may experience the pain through tasks.
- The docs reject folders, but users may need some retrieval structure.
- The docs value AI, but users may distrust generated summaries of sensitive work.
