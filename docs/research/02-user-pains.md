## Evidence base

These pains are extracted from repository documentation and treated as first-hand evidence from the founder's work.

## Pain clusters

### Memory pain

The founder loses details of what happened, why it happened, and what state the work was in.

### Recovery pain

The founder wastes energy returning to work after a context switch.

### Reporting pain

The founder struggles to explain progress because the evidence of work is distributed across tools.

### Handoff pain

The founder cannot hand off work cleanly when decisions and reasoning were never captured.

### Tool-friction pain

The founder works around tools that are optimized for tasks, projects, documentation, time records, and team process rather than personal professional memory.

## Strongest pain

The strongest documented pain is the gap between productive work and remembered work.

The founder does work, changes direction, answers questions, makes decisions, and moves between contexts. Later, the founder must reconstruct what happened from scattered traces.
